# Link-preview crawlers are explicitly ALLOWED. Blocking them disabled every
# OpenGraph card this site is built around: src/lib/seo.ts sets og:title,
# og:description and og:image per public page, and scripts/generate-og-image.mjs
# exists solely to produce the 1200x630 card. X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Instagram
# and LinkedIn all honour robots.txt, so a shared link rendered as a bare URL
# with no title and no image. They can only ever reach the public marketing
# pages — every member route is disallowed above, and those routes are
# client-rendered behind sign-in as well — so allowing them costs the
# "no public directory" guarantee nothing.
